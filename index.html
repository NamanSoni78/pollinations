The Trolley Problem, with Jev
a philosophy game for decision models and the people who feed them

A runaway trolley. A lever.
You — and Jev at the switch.

Ten dilemmas from sixty years of ethics. You lock in your choice first. Then Jev — a fast, cheap decision model — answers the very same question with calibrated probabilities instead of prose. Do you and the machine reach for the same lever?

Jev answers with a choice, a permissibility probability, and a seriousness score — every consultation paid with your own Pollen (bring your own Pollen sign-in), well under a cent per game.

The Lab

Bring your own Pollen

Sign-in uses Pollinations' bring-your-own-Pollen OAuth flow: you approve a scoped, budget-capped key and the game spends only your own balance. No key ever leaves your browser except as the request's Authorization header. You can revoke it any time from enter.pollinations.ai/keys.
